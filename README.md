## Favorite Series: *Severance*

> "The work is mysterious and important." — Lumon Industries

**Severance** is a sci-fi psychological thriller on Apple TV+ about employees whose memories are surgically divided between their work and personal lives.

### Why I Recommend It
* **Unique Premise:** Explores work-life balance in a literal, unsettling way.
* **Great Pacing:** Every episode builds tension toward an unforgettable season finale.
* **Cinematography:** Symmetrical framing and stark set design make every scene stand out.
